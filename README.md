# Buildings over What Have Been Built

An open dataset of heritage adaptive reuse case studies that honor the past and anticipate the future.

Historic buildings often outlive the purpose they were built for. Train stations become museums, factories become houses, churches become libraries. Buildings over What Have Been Built collects projects where heritage structures were given a new life, and reads each one closely: what the building was, what it became, and how the change was made.

The blog grew out of a master's thesis on the adaptive reuse of heritage buildings. Every project is analysed through the same framework, so cases can be compared across countries, centuries and building types instead of being read as isolated stories.

## What each case study covers

- **Function**: the building's original use and its new one
- **Form**: geometry and spatial organisation, before and after
- **Strategy**: the adaptive approaches applied to the building
- **Analysis**: five sections covering the introduction, reuse goal, challenge, adaptive strategy and outcome, illustrated with drawings and before-and-after comparisons
